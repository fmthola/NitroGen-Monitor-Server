Experimenting with AI that plays video games.

I've been working on getting NVIDIA's NitroGen gaming AI running on consumer hardware. This is a 500M parameter vision-action model trained on 40,000 hours of gameplay across 1,000+ games.

The goal? Dual-GPU gaming: Intel ARC handles the game, NVIDIA RTX handles AI inference. Keeping VRAM free for gaming while the AI does its thing.

**Current results on RTX 3070:**
- Running at 12-15 FPS with optimized settings
- Successfully driving vehicles around in Cyberpunk 2077
- Tracked and engaged an enemy (not perfectly, didn't survive the encounter)
- Definitely needs more tweaking

It's far from perfect. The AI gets confused, makes questionable decisions, and definitely won't be winning any competitive matches. But watching it attempt to play is fascinating.

Next steps: fine-tuning for specific games and adding task guidance so the AI can follow instructions.

This is very much a work in progress. Check out the project if you're interested in gaming AI:
https://github.com/fmthola/NitroGen-Monitor-Server
